## 🎮 About the Game

Big Ambitions is a single-player business simulation and role-playing game developed by Hovgaard Games. It entered Early Access on March 10, 2023, and reached full release on August 28, 2026. Players build an entrepreneurial empire in New York by managing businesses, property, staff, logistics, finances, vehicles, and personal needs.

---

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Big Ambitions**. This trainer provides quality-of-life controls for Big Ambitions, allowing players to adjust money, personal needs, time, inventory capacity, business stock, and operational resources. It is designed to reduce repetitive management tasks while preserving the game's business-building and life-simulation systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with business layouts, explore New York, test logistics systems, expand companies quickly, or reduce repetitive resource and time management in the single-player campaign.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Personal Funds** — Prevents the player's personal cash balance from decreasing, making it easier to purchase property, vehicles, furniture, food, and other necessities.
- **Unlimited Business Funds** — Protects selected business cash balances during expansion, purchasing, staffing, and daily operations.
- **Freeze Personal Needs** — Stops energy, hunger, health, and happiness needs from declining while the option is active.
- **Fast Time Control** — Freezes or accelerates the in-game clock to simplify travel, work schedules, deliveries, rest, and business planning.
- **Unlimited Business Stock** — Prevents selected store inventory from being depleted during sales and customer service.
- **Instant Business Operations** — Reduces waiting associated with selected business management tasks, restocking, and logistics actions.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Unlimited Inventory Capacity** — Removes practical limits from the player's carried inventory, helping with shopping, stocking businesses, and moving supplies.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **No Combat Mode** — Big Ambitions has no conventional combat system. This category is included for trainer compatibility, but no combat enhancements are required.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the trainer's time and resource options while traveling between businesses, apartments, suppliers, warehouses, dealerships, and other locations. The game supports movement through public transport, taxis, and owned vehicles, so use these options to reduce downtime without changing the normal world layout.

### Tools & Inventory

Inventory controls are useful when purchasing food, furniture, products, and business supplies. Activate unlimited capacity before collecting large quantities of goods, and disable it before saving if you want to return to normal inventory behavior.

### World & Environment

Big Ambitions is built around an open New York business environment. The trainer can simplify property purchases, vehicle ownership, business expansion, stock management, and daily expenses, allowing you to focus on opening stores, hiring staff, managing logistics, and building a corporate network.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no conventional combat gameplay in Big Ambitions. The trainer therefore provides no weapon, damage, or combat-related options.
